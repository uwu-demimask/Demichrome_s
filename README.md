# ◼ DEMICHROME
### A different relationship with your phone.

**Not another app. A quiet rebellion against digital noise.**

We spend hours inside our phones, jumping between notifications, endless feeds, and distractions we never intended to open.

Demichrome explores a different possibility: **what if your phone felt less like a slot machine and more like a tool you actually control?**

Built around monochrome aesthetics, intentional interactions, and a quieter digital experience, Demichrome is an experiment in taking back your attention without giving up the usefulness of your device.

---

### ◻ The idea

Technology should help you live your life, not compete with it.

Demichrome is being built around a simple philosophy:

- **Less noise.** Create space between yourself and digital distractions.
- **More intention.** Make every interaction feel deliberate.
- **Your device, your rules.** Shape your digital environment around what matters to you.
- **Function over decoration.** Every visual choice should have a purpose.

### ◻ The aesthetic

Inspired by monochrome computing, retro interfaces, and the restraint of modern industrial design.

No unnecessary clutter. No visual overload. Just a distinctive interface that feels purposeful, tactile, and unmistakably its own.

### ◻ The mission

Demichrome isn't about abandoning technology.

It's about using technology without letting it use you.

A small experiment in digital minimalism, built one interaction at a time.

### ◻ Project status

**Work in progress.**

Demichrome is actively being developed. Its design, implementation, and capabilities may evolve as the project takes shape.
